title: Licensing & Activation
description: How trials, activation, and licenses work depending on where you bought Darkwave.
---

How this works depends on where you got Darkwave — the Mac App Store version and the direct-download version are licensed differently.

## Mac App Store

If you bought Darkwave from the Mac App Store, there's nothing to activate. Your purchase, license, and updates are all handled by your Apple ID and the App Store, the same as any other Mac App Store app. Opening the app is proof of purchase.

## Direct download (macOS DMG or Windows installer)

The direct-download build starts a 14-day free trial automatically the first time it runs — every feature is available during the trial, with a countdown shown in the app. When the trial ends, you'll need a license key to keep using it.

**Activating a key**: enter the license key from your purchase email along with the email address you used to buy it. Activation is tied to that specific machine (identified by a hardware id, not anything personally identifying beyond the purchase email) — each key can be activated on **up to two machines** at once, so you can use Darkwave on both a desktop and a laptop, for instance.

**Moving to a new machine**: if you've already used both activations and need to move to a new machine, contact support (see the [Support](/darkwave/user-guide/support/) page) to have one freed up.

**Lost your key**: use "Resend my license key" on the activation screen, or the trial-expired screen, with the email address you purchased with — a fresh copy is emailed to you.

**Updates**: the direct-download build checks for updates automatically and installs them in the background; you don't need to manually re-download from the store page for routine updates.
