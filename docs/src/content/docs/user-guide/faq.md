---
title: FAQ
description: Common questions about Darkwave.
---

**What is Darkwave?** A local-first desktop app that turns imported sound effects and music into a searchable, reusable personal library — drop audio in once, find it instantly forever. See [Vision](/darkwave/product/vision/) for the full pitch.

**Does my audio leave my computer?** No. Your library — files, tags, notes, everything — stays on your machine or wherever you point it (including a NAS share). Darkwave has no cloud sync and no telemetry. See the [Privacy Policy](/darkwave/legal/privacy-policy/) for the exact details, which differ slightly between the Mac App Store and direct-download builds.

**What formats does it support?** WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF, and OGG play and get full analysis (waveform, tempo, tagging, similarity). AAC/M4A files can be imported, tagged, and organized, and still play back fine — analysis support for them is on the roadmap.

**What's the difference between the Mac App Store version and the direct download?** Same app. The Mac App Store version is sandboxed per Apple's requirements and licensed/updated entirely through your Apple ID. The direct download (macOS DMG or Windows installer) runs unsandboxed with full filesystem access, uses a 14-day trial plus a license key you buy directly, and updates itself in the background. See [Licensing & Activation](/darkwave/user-guide/licensing-and-activation/).

**Is Windows available on the Microsoft Store?** No — Windows is direct-download only.

**How does classification work?** Every sound gets one of six types — Soundtrack, Voiceover, Sound Effect, Foley, Ambience, or Other — either picked manually or auto-suggested from real analysis of the decoded audio (vocal presence, tempo, etc.), not just filename guessing.

**What's SONIC RADAR / "Find Similar"?** A similarity search that finds sonically related sounds in your library based on real audio analysis, not just shared tags.

**Can I use a NAS or external drive as my library's source?** Yes — you pick the source folder explicitly when you first import, and Darkwave handles reconnect if the share goes offline and comes back.

**How do exports work with projects?** Sending a track to a project nests it under a folder structure by media type (`01_MUSIC`, `02_VO`, `03_SFX`, `04_FOLEY`), with Foley further split by its primary tag.

**Is there a trial?** The direct-download build includes a 14-day free trial with every feature available. The Mac App Store build is a standard paid purchase with no separate trial.

**Where do I get help?** See [Support](/darkwave/user-guide/support/).
