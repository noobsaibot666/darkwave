---
title: System Requirements
description: Supported platforms and hardware for Darkwave.
---

**macOS**: macOS 12 (Monterey) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. Available from the Mac App Store or as a direct download.

**Windows**: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Available as a direct download; not distributed through the Microsoft Store.

Darkwave is a lightweight, local-first catalog — it doesn't need a powerful machine. 8 GB of RAM and a few hundred MB of free disk space for the app itself are enough; actual library size is bounded by the storage backing your media (which can live on an external drive or NAS share, not local disk).

An internet connection is only needed for the initial download, checking for updates on the direct-sale build, and activating a direct-sale license key. Everything else — importing, tagging, playback, search, export — works fully offline.
